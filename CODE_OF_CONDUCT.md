# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique the work without attacking
people. Welcome questions and different levels of experience. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

Keep reports relevant to Toastty and remove private terminal content. Maintainers
may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive conversations, or restrict
participation. For private concerns, use the contact method on the maintainer's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/dar7an); do not publish someone else's private
information while reporting a problem.
