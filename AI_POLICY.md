# AI-assisted contributions

AI assistance is welcome when disclosed. Contributors remain responsible for
understanding the change, checking its sources and license, reviewing generated
code, and verifying behavior in the running application. Include the tools used
and how the result was tested. Do not submit unverified bulk changes or invented
results. Do not include private prompts, credentials, or terminal history.

The initial Toastty workspace work and branding were developed with AI assistance.
Icon generation provenance is recorded in `docs/branding/README.md`.
